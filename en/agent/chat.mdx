Working with the agent is a conversation. You describe the result you want, the agent asks clarifying questions, and you refine the answers and add context as you go.

## How do I give the agent a task?

Write in chat what you want to get. The more specific, the better: say where the data comes from, what format the result should take and when to deliver it.

```text
Every morning at 9:00 send me a summary of unread Gmail emails
with a short description of each.
```

If the task needs a service, the agent offers to connect it — a sign-in button appears right in chat.

<Tip>
  Start with a real task you deal with often: finding new leads, tracking competitors, preparing reports, monitoring email or summarizing updates across tools.
</Tip>

## Can I attach files, images and voice?

Yes. In the message box you can:

- **Upload a file** — a document, spreadsheet or PDF
- **Paste an image** — a screenshot, photo or diagram
- **Dictate by voice** — to explain the task faster

Uploaded files are saved to the agent's workspace. More in [Files and results](/en/agent/files).

## What are the suggestions in the message box?

The longer you work together, the better ASCN's suggestions get: it offers ready-made prompts, the connectors you need and workflows based on the current conversation. Suggestions appear right in the message box, adapt to your language and refresh when you need a different starting point.

## How do I teach the agent to work my way?

There are three ways, and they complement each other:

- **Say it in chat** — "Remember: I prefer answers as a list". The agent saves it to [memory](/en/brain/memory).
- **Write rules in Knowledge** — role, style and limits in IDENTITY.md, SOUL.md and USER.md. More in [Knowledge](/en/brain/knowledge).
- **Create a skill** — a repeatable behaviour for a typical task. More in [Skills](/en/brain/skills).

## Where else can I message the agent?

Besides ASCN web chat, you can message the agent in [Telegram](/en/channels/telegram) and [Slack](/en/channels/slack). It also sends notifications from scheduled tasks and triggers there.

To integrate with your own systems, use the [API](/en/settings/api): each conversation is a session with its own `session_id`.
