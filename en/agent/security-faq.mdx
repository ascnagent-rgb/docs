## Can other agents see my data?

No. Each agent runs in a separate isolated workspace — one agent's data isn't available to another.

## Is my data used to train models?

No. The agent doesn't use your data to train AI models and doesn't share it with other users.

## How are passwords, tokens and keys stored?

Encrypted. [Secret](/en/settings/secrets) values aren't shown in the interface after saving and don't appear in chat history or logs. Standard integrations (Gmail, Google Calendar, Slack, etc.) sign in through OAuth — their passwords are never passed to the agent.

## Can the agent send an email without me knowing?

No. By default the agent prepares a draft and asks for confirmation before sending. Pin this rule in SOUL.md or IDENTITY.md — knowledge rules take priority over offhand phrases in chat. More in [Memory](/en/brain/memory).

## Can the agent delete my files?

Only if you've turned on **Allow file deletion** in [tool permissions](/en/settings/permissions).

## What does the agent get when I connect my personal Telegram account?

Access to your chats and messages, and the ability to send messages on your behalf by command or trigger. Without an explicit command or a configured trigger, the agent doesn't reply in chats. To keep things separate, use a dedicated Telegram account. More in [Telegram](/en/channels/telegram).

## How do I revoke the agent's access to a service?

Open **Brain → Integrations** and disable or remove the connection. Note that tasks using this service will stop working. More in [Apps and integrations](/en/brain/connectors).

## How do I delete all of the agent's data?

**Settings → Danger zone → Delete workspace**. Chats, Brain settings, tasks, channels, files and secrets are permanently deleted. More in [Settings and notifications](/en/settings/delete-agent).
