ASCN Agent is a proactive AI agent with its own workspace. Describe a task in plain words — the agent connects to the services it needs, does the work and sends the result to chat, Telegram or Slack. It can be your personal assistant, support specialist, marketer or analyst, depending on what you hand it.

## Agents that work for you

Create one agent or several — one for each role. Agents work inside your apps: they read email, manage your calendar, watch files and post messages. Talk to them in chat, attach files and images, or dictate a task by voice.

The agent connects to Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, Notion, HubSpot and 3000+ other services, plus any of your own tools through MCP or the [API](/en/settings/api).
