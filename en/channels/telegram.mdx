Telegram is the most convenient way to interact with the agent outside of the browser: get notifications, ask questions, give commands right from the messenger.

## DM with a bot

### Step 1. Create a bot via BotFather

Open [@BotFather](https://t.me/BotFather) in Telegram and create a new bot. ASCN shows step-by-step guidance in the UI.

![BotFather dialog — /newbot, name, username, token](/images/en/10-botfather.png)

### Step 2. Follow the ASCN wizard

Open **Channels → Telegram**. ASCN launches a connection wizard:

1. Open BotFather in Telegram
2. Send `/newbot`
3. Pick a display name
4. Pick a username (must end with `_bot`)
5. Copy the token — BotFather will send it in chat

![Telegram connection wizard in ASCN](/images/en/11-1-telegram-wizard.png)

### Step 3. Paste the token

Click **I have a token →**, paste the token. You can paste the entire BotFather message — ASCN extracts the token automatically.

Click **Connect bot**.

![Token input and «Connect bot» button](/images/en/11-2-telegram-token.png)

### Step 4. Verify ownership

After connecting, ASCN asks to verify the bot:

1. Click **Open bot in Telegram**
2. Click **Start** in the dialog — it links your account with the agent

![Verify: open bot and click Start](/images/en/11-3-telegram-verify.png)

### Done

After clicking Start the bot greets you and offers to connect integrations right in Telegram.

![Bot in Telegram after /start](/images/en/11-4-telegram-bot.png)

## Group or channel

You can add the agent to a Telegram group or channel — for team work or publishing notifications. Add the bot as administrator, get the chat ID (e.g. via [@RawDataBot](https://t.me/RawDataBot)) and enter it in ASCN.

## Personal account

The agent can also work via your personal Telegram account — not as a bot, but as you.

<Warning>
  When connecting a personal account the agent gets access to your chats and can send messages on your behalf via commands or triggers. The agent does NOT auto-reply without an explicit command or configured trigger.
</Warning>

## What you can do via Telegram

- Ask questions and get answers
- Give commands: «send an email», «book a meeting», «what do I have today?»
- Get notifications from triggers and scheduled tasks
- Upload files and images right in chat
