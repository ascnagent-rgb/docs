Slack lets you talk to the agent right inside your team workspace — ask questions, get notifications, give commands without leaving your environment.

## How to connect Slack

1. Open **Channels** in the agent settings
2. Pick **Slack**
3. Click **Connect**
4. Sign in to your Slack account and choose a workspace
5. Grant the requested permissions

After connecting, the agent appears in Slack as an app.

## What you can do via Slack

- Ask questions and get answers in DM with the agent
- Give commands: «check email», «what's on my calendar?», «send me a summary»
- Get trigger and scheduled task notifications
- Send messages to channels through the agent (when the Slack integration is connected in Brain)

<Info>
  To post to Slack channels through the agent, also connect the **Slack integration** in **Brain → Integrations**.
</Info>
