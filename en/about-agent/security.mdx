## Principles

- **Each agent runs separately** — one agent's data isn't available to another
- **Passwords and keys are stored encrypted** — nobody but you has access to them
- **You decide what the agent can do** — sending emails, deleting files, creating events: each action requires your permission
- **Access can be revoked at any time** — disconnect any service or delete the agent completely

## What the agent doesn't do without your permission

- Doesn't send emails
- Doesn't share your data with other users
- Doesn't use your data to train AI models
