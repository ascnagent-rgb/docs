---
title: "What is ASCN Agent?"
description: "An AI employee that works for you in the background — while you're busy with other things."
---

ASCN Agent is an AI assistant you configure once for your tasks and tools. After that, it works on its own: monitors events, runs scheduled scenarios, and communicates with you wherever it's convenient — in chat, Telegram, or Slack.

## What the agent can do

A regular chatbot answers questions. ASCN Agent **acts**:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Never misses anything important" icon="bell">
    Monitors your email, calendar, and files — and automatically responds to the right events.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Works without reminders" icon="robot">
    Runs scenarios on schedule or on a trigger — without your involvement.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Knows everything you know" icon="database">
    Works with your files and knowledge base — answers questions, analyzes data, generates reports.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Keeps you informed" icon="paper-plane">
    Sends daily digests and notifications wherever you'll see them.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Connected to your tools" icon="plug">
    Works with Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Slack, GitHub, and more.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Finds up-to-date information" icon="magnifying-glass">
    Searches the web and analyzes results on request or on schedule.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## How the agent is structured

The agent has three parts:

1. **Brain** — where you define what the agent is connected to, how it behaves, and what it knows about you
2. **Tasks** — automations on schedule and triggered by events
3. **Channels** — where the agent communicates with you: ASCN, Telegram, Slack
